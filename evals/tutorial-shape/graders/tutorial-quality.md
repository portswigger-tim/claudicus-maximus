PASS if the reply says at the start what the reader will achieve, gives one clear path with numbered steps, shows the expected result after the key steps, and ends with a short pointer to what to read next. A short definition of a term for a beginner (a sentence or two), and commands shown for more than one operating system, are fine.
FAIL if it offers several alternative methods (for example pip vs poetry vs conda), spends several paragraphs or a whole section on how virtual environments work before the steps, or leaves the reader without a way to check that it worked.
